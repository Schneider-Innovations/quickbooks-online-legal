SND Mirror Sender is an internal tool of Schneider Innovations Group, LLC and is not offered to the public.

Only an authorized QuickBooks Online company admin of a Schneider Innovations company connects it, after finance management has approved that company for mirroring, following the company's internal authorization procedure.

To reconnect after a disconnection or an expired authorization, the same admin repeats that authorization.
